Listening kit: Krtek

About 15 minutes: eight short clips from the two films Vadim Petrov scored (Hodinářem, 1974; Buldozer, 1975), about 8 minutes of film plus time to write. For each one, press ▶ Play the clip (it stops at the end), listen, and jot short answers. "Don't know" is a fine answer, and so is a hum written down as "da-da-DUM". Your answers become observations in worlds/krtek/world.yaml, and the meadow's music direction is written from them. They stay in this browser; Download answers saves them for Claude.

The films aren't here: they live in worlds/krtek/media/ (not in git). Fetch them as info.txt there says.
